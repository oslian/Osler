snapshot trials failed stable glucose benefit. Current native event contract,
independent source evaluation and hospital roles have not tested it.

shuffle forbids same-patient donors and uses fit labels only. Same32epochs,
16x32patient draws, AdamW1e-4, identity output and source-tune glucose selection.
No retention to inaccurate initial outputs. No new architecture or tuning.
Evaluate all supported targets vs original, initial, persistence; compare real
and shuffled afterward. Success needs actual generalization, not in-fit loss.

remain. No runtime, interval, clinical or causal promotion from this experiment.
