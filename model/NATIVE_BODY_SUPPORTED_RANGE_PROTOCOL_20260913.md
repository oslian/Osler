# Numeric Summary Support Guard

Fixed exploratory threshold20 fit patients. Latest/mean require >=1event for
20patients; std/slope require >=2events for20patients. Same original patients,
source-only range bound, source-tune ridge/population selection. Unsupported
numeric columns are zeroed in all roles, support columns retained. Apply same
mask to placebo. Compare against bounded, support, population and placebo.
Keep all heldout patients; no alternative thresholds selected after results.
20 is a prespecified engineering guard, not a statistical sufficiency theorem.
May suppress true but rare physiology. No clinical/promotion claim is implied.
