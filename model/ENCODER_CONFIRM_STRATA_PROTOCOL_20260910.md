# Descriptive Repair Subgroups

This follow-up was specified after training started and before subgroup results
were read. It is not a prospective temporal-holdout promotion gate.
All eICU unit types/hospital-discharge years and MIMIC first care units/patient
anchor_year_group periods are enumerated. At least30 distinct patients are
required for a subgroup CI; missing metadata is unsupported. Every group,
including insufficient groups, is retained. Fixed weights and fixed source
selection only; no calibration or threshold selection from these outcomes.
Patient-paired95% bootstrap CIs use1000 draws. Multiple comparisons are
exploratory, unadjusted. Direction accuracy is patient-equal; delta correlation
is row-weighted descriptive, not a promotion claim. Period groups are coarse
provenance, not exact unshifted MIMIC patient dates.
