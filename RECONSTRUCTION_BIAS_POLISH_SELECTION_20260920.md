## Decision

The source-fit readout optimization gap is real, but the proposed bias-polish
repair is not supported for common two-group deployment. Stop sweeping offsets,
slopes, shrinkage or acceptance thresholds on these same outcomes. This is a
rejected universal calibration repair, not proof all calibration is impossible,
nor proof all33targets lack signal. The remaining problem concerns transferable
conditional representation/readout information; earlier raw-state and learned
readout experiments must be reviewed before another capacity change.
